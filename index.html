Welcome!

We are glad you are here

A Pack to Recovery is our church family's way of meeting neighbors where they are. Each backpack carries everyday essentials for today, and a printed card with phone numbers and a QR code to this guide for tomorrow.

Help is offered freely. No one has to attend church, share their story, or meet any condition to receive a backpack or use this directory.

The packing list

What's in each backpack

First aid

Bandages, gauze, antiseptic wipes and blister care for long days on foot.

Personal care

Toothbrush, toothpaste, soap, deodorant, wipes, lip balm and hygiene items.

Food & water

Bottled water and soft, easy-open, shelf-stable food.

Phone charging

A solar power bank and cable, so a phone can reach 2-1-1, family or a job.

Warmth

New socks, gloves, hat and an emergency blanket as the season requires.

Resource card

A two-sided card with key numbers and a QR code to this guide.

Pathway to recovery

Today, tomorrow, and the next step

  1. Meet today's need

    A meal, water, a warm place, a charged phone. Use the directory to find what's open.

  2. Make one connection

    Call 2-1-1 or one provider. Ask what to bring and where to go. A volunteer can help you make the call.

  3. Choose what's next

    Housing, health care, recovery, a job or an ID — at your own pace, with people who care.
