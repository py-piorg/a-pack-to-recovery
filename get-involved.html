Packing list

What goes in every backpack

Print this list and take it shopping. Travel sizes keep packs light.

The bag

  • Sturdy backpack, dark color
  • Gallon zip bags to keep items dry
  • Resource card (we print these)

Hygiene

  • Toothbrush & toothpaste
  • Bar soap or body wash
  • Deodorant
  • Wet wipes & tissues
  • Lip balm, comb
  • Feminine products (separate bag)

First aid

  • Adhesive bandages
  • Gauze & tape
  • Antiseptic wipes
  • Blister pads
  • Hand sanitizer

Food & water

  • 2 bottles of water
  • Soft granola or protein bars
  • Peanut butter crackers
  • Pop-top tuna or chicken
  • Fruit cups (pull-tab), applesauce pouches

Warmth & comfort

  • 2 pairs of new wool or thick socks
  • Knit hat and gloves
  • Emergency mylar blanket
  • Hand warmers (winter)
  • Rain poncho

Connection

  • Solar or battery power bank
  • USB-C and Lightning cable
  • Small notebook & pen
  • An encouraging handwritten note

Please avoid: glass, razors, mouthwash containing alcohol, medications, and anything expired.

Drive timeline

How the drive works

  1. Collect

    Supplies and sponsorships come in from our church family and community.

  2. Verify

    Volunteers call listings to confirm resource card numbers are current.

  3. Pack

    Packing day: assembly line, prayer, and a handwritten note in every bag.

  4. Share

    Backpacks go out with outreach partners, with dignity and respect.

For volunteers

Handing out with dignity

Lead with respect

Introduce yourself, ask their name, make eye contact. Offer, don't insist. “Would a backpack help today?”

Listen first

Ask what they need most right now. Point to the card and this site. Offer to help make one call.

Stay safe and together

Go in pairs, stay in public areas, and follow partner guidance. If someone is in danger, call 911.
