What to do if…

Your situation, your next step

Someone is not breathing or may be overdosing

Call 911. Give naloxone (Narcan) if you have it. Connecticut’s Good Samaritan law protects people who call for help during an overdose.

You need a place to sleep tonight

Call 2-1-1 and press 3. Every Greater Hartford shelter bed is reached through 2-1-1. In cold weather, ask about warming centers.

You are thinking about suicide or feel unsafe

Call or text 988. For a crisis team that comes to you, call 2-1-1, press 1, then 2. Hartford adults: 860-297-0999.

You are not safe at home

CT Safe Connect: 888-774-2900 (call, text or chat). Confidential, 24/7, with emergency DV shelter.

You want help with drugs or alcohol

CT Access Line: 800-563-4086, 24/7, for detox and treatment, with rides. Free Narcan: CT Harm Reduction Alliance 860-272-1037.

You need medical care

Charter Oak Health Care for the Homeless: 860-550-7500. Emergency rooms are open 24/7 at Hartford Hospital and Saint Francis.

Calling 2-1-1

What to expect

  • 2-1-1 is free and answers 24/7 in many languages.
  • For shelter, press 3. Specialists can schedule a Coordinated Access appointment.
  • Tell them if you have children, a disability, a pet, or are fleeing violence.
  • No phone? Hartford Public Library (500 Main St) and day centers can help you call.
  • If 211 doesn't connect, dial 800-203-1234.
