Our heart

Why A Pack to Recovery

Our communities continue to face difficult and challenging circumstances. As a church, we want to be there for our neighbors to bring comfort, hope and practical help.

Homelessness in Connecticut has risen in recent years, and winter in Hartford County is hard. A backpack can't solve everything, but it can meet today's need with dignity and point toward tomorrow's help. That's why every pack carries a resource card, and why this website exists.

Questions

FAQ

Do I have to belong to the church to get a backpack?

No. Backpacks and this directory are free for anyone. There are no religious requirements and no questions you have to answer.

How do I get a backpack?

Backpacks are handed out with local outreach partners. Contact the church to ask about upcoming distribution, or to refer someone.

Is this directory complete?

It lists 131 programs in Hartford County and statewide, but programs change. 2-1-1 has the most current information for every program in Connecticut.

Can I suggest a resource or report a change?

Yes, please! Contact the church with the program name, phone and website.

Can I print this directory?

Yes. On the Find Help page, filter or star what you need, then choose “Print this list.” There is also a two-sided printable handout.

How can my church, business or group help?

Sponsor backpacks, run a supply drive, or volunteer as a group on packing day. Visit Get Involved.
